Each turn ends with a SPATIAL GROUNDING block that the harness computed from the observation and
from where you have already been. Trust it: every compass move is listed with what lies on that
square, how many times you have stood there, and whether it is RULED OUT (lethal terrain, a wall,
or a move the game already refused from this exact square). A ruled-out move will not be executed.
Prefer squares you have visited fewer times, and follow the `recommended` move unless the
observation shows a better reason (for example an adjacent staircase down `>` or a monster to fight).
